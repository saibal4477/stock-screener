The Silent Flip Strategy — Institutional Supply & Resistance Fading

Created by Karl Eriksson (ProRealAlgos) and quantitatively backtested across 6,484 S&P 500 setups. The strategy targets the opening 15–45 minute morning volatility surge by fading key structural daily resistance levels when volatility contracts into a Silent Candle.

📐 Strategy Anatomy & Visual Level Diagram

How key levels, the silent candle, entry, stop, and targets align

Target R:R ≥ 1.5x Required
1️⃣Key Level Verification

Price must test either Range High (RH) or Flip High (FH) during the morning session. The candle must respect the level without closing beyond a 0.3% breakout tolerance threshold.

2️⃣The Silent Candle Trigger

Must form right at the level. Total candle range must be ≤ 60% of ATR(14) and body must close inside prior candle. This confirms institutional order absorption and breakout exhaustion.

3️⃣Execution & Asymmetry

Sell Stop 1 tick below Silent Candle Low. Stop Loss 1 tick above High. Distance to opposite Range Low target must be ≥ 1.5× initial risk. Shift stop to breakeven at +1R. Close all trades at 3:55 PM EST.

💡 The 3 Discoveries from 6,484 S&P 500 Trade Backtests

1. Shorts Have Positive Expectancy: Shorting Range High & Flip High generated +127.10 R. Buying Range Low lost -275.14 R due to morning opening breakdown cascades.
2. Defensive / Financial Dominance: Financials, REITs, Utilities, and Staples delivered +142.78 R. Tech momentum stocks trend strongly through levels and should be avoided.
3. The 1.5x R:R Edge Multiplier: Requiring target distance ≥ 1.5× risk produced a 71.0% win rate and a 5.37 profit factor in the curated 30-stock portfolio.
